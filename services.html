Services

One service, done properly: your books

Bookkeeping is all we do. Here is what that covers, and what it means for your week.

No. 01

Day-to-day bookkeeping

Sales, purchases and payments recorded as they happen, bank accounts reconciled and receipts filed where they can be found again. Whether your records live in software or a shoebox today, we set up a routine that keeps them current without eating your evenings.

No. 02

BAS-ready records

GST tracked correctly through the quarter so activity statement time is a formality, not a scramble. Your accountant gets clean figures, deadlines are diarised in advance and you always know what is due and when.

No. 03

Payroll

Wages calculated, superannuation tracked and payslips issued on schedule. Your staff get paid correctly and on time, every time, and the records behind each pay run stay audit-tidy.

No. 04

Reporting you can read

Regular summaries in plain language: what came in, what went out, who owes you and what is coming up. Numbers you can act on, not a report you file away unread.

No. 05

Payment services and MYOB

We run payment services for our clients and work in MYOB day to day. If you are not set up yet, we can get you started on the right plan.

Not sure what your business needs? Ring us and describe how you handle the books today. We will tell you honestly what we would take off your plate.
