About the practice

Twenty years of keeping the record straight

No. 01 · The story

A local practice, built on repeat clients

P A & D E Bookkeeping Services has kept small-business books for twenty years, most of them in Albury, where the practice still runs from 433 Wagga Road, Lavington. We opened on Berry Street in Nowra six months ago, bringing the same service to the Shoalhaven. The work has not changed in that time: record things accurately, keep clients informed and never let a deadline sneak up on anyone.

Most of our clients have been with us for years, and most new clients arrive by word of mouth. In towns like Albury and Nowra, that is the only advertising that counts.

No. 02 · How we work

Three habits from twenty years of practice

Accuracy first

A ledger is only useful if every line in it is right. We check our work, then check it again before it reaches you or your accountant.

Plain answers

You should never leave a conversation about your own books more confused than when you started. We explain things in ordinary language.

On the phone, in person

We are a local office, not a call centre. Ring during business hours and you speak to the person doing your books.

Come and meet us in Nowra or Albury
